MatchForge

Backend, real-time, event-driven

Real-time matchmaking and lobby service: skill-based matching with expanding Elo/MMR windows, JWT with refresh-token rotation, and SignalR lobbies with reconnect and win-by-forfeit. Race conditions solved with Redis distributed locks; 2 replicas behind nginx with a SignalR Redis backplane. k6 load test: 1,000 concurrent players, p95 match time 2.0 s, 0% failed requests. Match events flow to RabbitMQ through a transactional outbox, and a separate stats service consumes them idempotently via an inbox to maintain a leaderboard, with retries and dead-letter queues.
